## What the observations can establish

| Boundary | Observation | Meaning and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Xbox capture / encoder / sender queue | Not available | RTP timestamp is media time, not verified capture time. |
| Wire / Wi-Fi / Vita driver arrival | Not available | The approved API has no established hardware-tested packet-arrival timestamp facility. No timestamp is fabricated. |
| UDP socket | Successful `try_recv_from` completion D and the latest independent non-consuming EAGAIN probe E, stamped **before** its syscall | Per-packet socket residence is in `[0, D-E]`. Before the first verified empty check it is unknown. A successful peek, readiness-cache WouldBlock, zero-byte UDP datagram, or probe error is never interpreted as an empty socket. Bounds do not cover driver/Wi-Fi queues before this socket. |
| RTC output | Original D and authenticated RTP delivery L | `L-D` is directly measured for the same packet. |
| RTP reorder output | D, L, ordered delivery O, SSRC/seq/RTP | `O-L` is directly measured for the same packet. |
| H.264 / AU / decoder / display | Existing AU and frame timing records retained alongside packet events | Actual assembly interval and first-dequeue-to-AU/submit ages remain distinct. Downstream frame-only identities are attributed only to a unique captured epoch/track/SSRC/RTP cohort. Ambiguous identity stays unknown. GPU completion is not physical scanout. |
| Audio | Authenticated audio RTP, its original D/L, clock rate, audio trace and status | Independent audio progression and local buffering remain visible. SDL enqueue is not audible output. |

For each authentic packet, the analyzer matches **epoch + SSRC + sequence + RTP timestamp + original dequeue Instant** against exactly one UDP observation. UDP header identities are explicitly untrusted until matched to RTC authentication. Missing or duplicate matches stay unknown; no neighbor's socket bound is substituted. The 16-bit sequence and 32-bit RTP values remain raw in the bundle. Analysis uses modular forward progression, ignores same-frame fragments/duplicates and late timestamps, separates stream/track opens, flags large unannounced discontinuities, and checks available RTP/NTP sender-report rates. It does not join across resets or assume that a missing sample had zero residence. An undetected small same-SSRC timestamp restart remains a clock/protocol uncertainty, not proof of physical capture age.

Let the same-packet dequeue/media displacement grow by `delta` relative to a baseline packet. If the delayed packet has socket upper bound U, the additional displacement **before this UDP socket** is at least `delta-U`, because the baseline socket residence cannot be negative. A small U can rule out the UDP queue as the source of most of a multi-second increase. A large U cannot establish a queue as its cause. The analyzer reports positive boundary evidence only for repeated observations over at least 500 ms; their coverage and largest observation gap are reported. It never asserts that missing intervening packets behaved identically. Relative RTP progression assumes the negotiated media rate; sender-report disagreement blocks attribution.

The outcomes are `BOUNDARY_EVIDENCE_OBTAINED`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, or `NOT REPRODUCED DURING CAPTURE`. None means “fixed.” A constant delay already present at startup is not measurable from a relative RTP baseline. Pre-socket delay cannot distinguish Xbox, LAN/Wi-Fi or driver delay. The runtime summary label “arrival” in older logs must not be read as an actual network-arrival measurement.

## First incident retention and budget

The device histories in `INGRESS-BOUNDARY-EVIDENCE.md` cross 500 ms at approximately 19 and 94 seconds. The later onset adds about 455 ms/s while audio stays close to its media clock. Accordingly, the **capture-only** trigger is 250 ms of added video RTP-to-application displacement, sustained across forward timestamp samples for 500 ms. It does not alter any media decision or request any recovery. This starts retaining the onset before the old 500 ms warning threshold. It observes the failing pre-decode pattern; absence of this pattern is not a test of all possible display/audio failures.

The recorder preallocates metadata storage and retains the first 3 seconds of video, a rolling previous 12 seconds, and the next 10 seconds after the first trigger. The incident then freezes permanently. A 180-second limit from first video freezes a no-reproduction capture; a trigger just before that limit still gets its full 10 seconds. The UI reports recording, collecting, complete, no reproduction, or incomplete. The compact capture status is independent of the existing diagnostics toggle.

There are 16,384 initial, 49,152 preceding and 49,152 following records, each at most 64 bytes: **7 MiB maximum metadata capacity**, plus at most 48 status strings capped at 8 KiB each (**384 KiB**) and small control structures. This is additional to the existing recorder/history. Time coverage is subject to this fixed capacity; overflowing metadata, truncated status text and lock misses are explicit, never silent. At 4,000 events/s, 12 seconds fits the preceding capacity. A faster burst can shorten it, but the startup copy remains independently retained. Post-trigger overflow freezes an explicitly incomplete incident without overwriting its onset.

Each packet hook is O(1), copies fixed metadata, and uses `try_lock`; decoder/display cannot wait on the capture lock. No packet payload copies, synchronous packet disk writes, threads, priorities or socket options are added. Frozen captures bypass locking. Existing non-consuming empty checks are unchanged. Host release-mode measurement of one million production recorder calls is printed in the host gate (approximately 40–70 ns/call on this execution host during development). This is **not a Vita measurement**. Vita timer, lock, memory-bandwidth, syscall and UI overhead remain unverified. The captured receive-pass durations, between-pass gaps, IO-call intervals and loss counts will expose interference but cannot retrospectively provide an instrumentation-free hardware baseline. Between-pass gaps include normal idle waiting and other work; they do not prove CPU starvation.

Saving runs on normal RTC ownership teardown after decoder shutdown. Four files are streamed to a `.partial` folder; successful sync/close and directory rename publish the final folder. Completed and partial earlier evidence are never overwritten by later sessions or delayed exports. A failed save is visible and retains available partial files. Normal Home exit uses the existing Home detach guard; it does not issue DELETE, start another game, power off, or require refresh/reconnect. Forced process termination or power loss before exit cannot save RAM. After a successful save, the first bundle is immutable even across app restart.

## If a second observation becomes necessary

The current approved Vita headers list `SO_RCVBUF`, receive timeout and peek facilities, but do not establish a usable per-datagram `SO_TIMESTAMP` arrival contract. No undocumented socket option, kernel plugin or driver change is included. Sources: https://github.com/vitasdk/vita-headers/blob/master/include/psp2common/net.h and the previously inspected newlib mapping documented in `INGRESS-BOUNDARY-EVIDENCE.md`. Header inspection cannot prove that every proprietary facility is impossible; none is currently validated for this pinned runtime.

If the returned bounds are large/unknown, the minimum next observation is **the same packet at a known external interface** during the same short incident. If they exclude the UDP queue but leave pre-socket growth, the external observation is also required to divide that earlier interval. Reuse this VPK and its capture schema. Do not start another generic acceptance matrix.

Prepared procedure for an engineer with an existing AP/router capture or a managed-switch mirror: mirror the Xbox's wired egress to a capture laptop if accessible; otherwise capture the AP's LAN ingress and explicitly retain that different observation-point identity. Run `tcpdump -i <mirror-or-LAN-interface> -s 128 -w greenvita-observer.pcap 'udp and host <Vita-IP>'` just before the same at-most-three-minute Home session. Stop after the capture; retain tcpdump's dropped-packet count and interface/position. Use a capture path known to include hardware-offloaded traffic; an empty router capture is not evidence of no packets. No TLS interception, credentials or video recording is required. The 128-byte snap length may include a short encrypted payload prefix; only header identities are used by the analyzer.

`tools/correlate_observer.py bundle.zip greenvita-observer.pcap --point 'Xbox wired egress mirror'` is prepared and parser-tested. It joins unique SSRC/seq/RTP identities and reports differences of elapsed times, cancelling constant clock offset. Relative clock skew/steps and capture loss still require examination using concurrent audio and clock health; the script does not guess a cause. Growth already present at immediate Xbox wire egress narrows the question to sender/media-clock/feedback behavior before that point. Growth added between it and UDP dequeue narrows it to the intervening network/driver/socket path. An AP ingress observation cannot separate the radio hop from the Vita driver. To separate those two would require a closer validated driver/air observation, not relabeling the AP timestamp as Vita arrival. No second capture is required in the initial user procedure.
